# News Management System

The News Management System allows Acme administrators to publish company updates, announcements, and blog posts directly from the dashboard.

## Accessing the CMS

1.  Log in to the **Dashboard**.
2.  Navigate to the **News** tab in the sidebar navigation.

## Creating a Post

Click the **"Create News +"** button to open the post editor. Fill in the following fields:

- **Title**: The headline of your post.
- **Slug**: The URL-friendly identifier (e.g., `q1-results-2026`).
- **Excerpt**: A short teaser that appears on the news feed grid.
- **Content**: The body of your post. Supports full **Markdown** and **GFM** (GitHub Flavored Markdown).
- **Status**: Choose between `Draft` (private) and `Published` (publicly visible).

## Markdown Support

The editor supports a wide range of markdown features:

| Feature                     | Syntax         |
| :-------------------------- | :------------- |
| **Bold**                    | `**text**`     |
| _Italic_                    | `*text*`       |
| [Links](https://google.com) | `[label](url)` |
| `Code`                      | `` `text` ``   |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> A post will only appear at `/news` if its status is explicitly set to **Published**.
